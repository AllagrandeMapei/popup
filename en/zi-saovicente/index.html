Cape St Vincent TSS

At the south western tip of Portugal, ships change course to enter or leave the Strait of Gibraltar, gateway to the Mediterranean. Routes here converge and cross instead of running parallel, and the TSS brings order to this junction. For the fleet it is a landmark mainly in editions when the weather pushes boats along the Iberian coast before heading down to the Canaries.

Category: Maritime trafficCorridors where merchant ships travel in lanes separated by direction, like on a motorway. During the race they are closed to the boats.
