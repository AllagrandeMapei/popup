Saint-Nazaire wind farm, Guérande bank

Off the Loire estuary, on the Guérande bank, stands France’s first commercial offshore wind farm: 80 turbines, in service since 2022. It is far from the direct route to Guadeloupe. It matters for boats heading down the French coast in the Bay of Biscay, for example to shelter from a storm or make a technical stop.

Category: Energy infrastructureOffshore wind farms and test sites. Turbines, cables and safety zones around the structures make them off limits to racing boats.
