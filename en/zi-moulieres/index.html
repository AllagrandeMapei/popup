Mussel bed zone

Moulières are mussel beds, natural or farmed, in shallow coastal waters. They are valuable ecosystems and an important resource for local fishing and mussel farming. Anchors, keels and daggerboards can damage them, while stakes, lines and nets are a hazard for boats. This is why sailing there is prohibited during the race, especially in the first hours, when the fleet is still close to the coast.

Category: Biodiversity protectionAreas where navigation is regulated to protect fragile ecosystems, cetaceans and sea turtles. Some are closed to the fleet, others flag zones to know and respect.
