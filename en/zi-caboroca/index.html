Traffic separation scheme

Cabo da Roca TSS

Cabo da Roca is the westernmost point of mainland Europe. Here the traffic heading down the Portuguese coast crosses paths with ships bound for the ports of Lisbon, Setúbal and Sines. The TSS separates these flows and prevents dangerous crossings close to the coast. It mainly affects boats that choose a route close to the Iberian Peninsula to look for more favourable wind before heading south west.

Category: Maritime trafficCorridors where merchant ships travel in lanes separated by direction, like on a motorway. During the race they are closed to the boats.
