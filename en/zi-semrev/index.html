SEM-REV test site

Off Le Croisic, SEM-REV is an offshore test site for marine renewable energy, run by the École Centrale de Nantes. Floating wind turbines and wave energy systems are tested here, connected to the grid by subsea cables. It is home to Floatgen, France’s first floating wind turbine. Measurement buoys, moorings and cables make it a no-go zone.
