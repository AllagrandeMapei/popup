Cape Finisterre TSS

Off Galicia, this is where shipping passes between Northern Europe and the Mediterranean, Africa and the Americas. This coast is known as the Costa da Morte, the “Coast of Death”, for its shipwrecks, including the oil tanker Prestige in 2002. To reduce the risks, the lanes have been moved further and further offshore. The fleet gets here after the Bay of Biscay, often in a big sea. Passing inside or outside the TSS is a strategic choice that can gain or lose a lot of miles.

Category: Maritime trafficCorridors where merchant ships travel in lanes separated by direction, like on a motorway. During the race they are closed to the boats.
