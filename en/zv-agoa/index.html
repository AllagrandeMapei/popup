Agoa sanctuary watch zone

Agoa is the marine mammal sanctuary of the French West Indies, created in 2010. It covers the waters of Guadeloupe, Martinique, Saint-Martin and Saint-Barthélemy, where more than twenty species of cetaceans live or pass through.

Category: Biodiversity protectionAreas where navigation is regulated to protect fragile ecosystems, cetaceans and sea turtles. Some are closed to the fleet, others flag zones to know and respect.
