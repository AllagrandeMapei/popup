Traffic separation scheme

Canary Islands East TSS

In the channel between Gran Canaria and Fuerteventura, the TSS separates ships heading north and south along the African coast. It was created in the mid 2000s, when the IMO recognised the Canary Islands as a particularly sensitive sea area. Its waters are home to cetaceans, turtles and fragile seabeds, exposed to the risk of collisions and pollution. It affects boats that choose the southern route to reach the trade winds sooner.
