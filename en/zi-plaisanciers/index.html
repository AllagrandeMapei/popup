Race safety

Start exclusion zone

On start day, thousands of spectator boats crowd the waters off Saint-Malo. The maritime prefecture has therefore divided the race area into zones: the racing zone is reserved for competitors, support RIBs and race management, while the others are open at set times to passenger ferries and motor boaters. Sailing, jet skis, fishing and anchoring are prohibited.

Category: Race safetyZones set up at the start and finish to protect skippers and the public.
