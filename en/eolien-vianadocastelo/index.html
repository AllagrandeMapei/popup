Viana do Castelo wind farm

About 20 kilometres off the north coast of Portugal, WindFloat Atlantic is one of Europe’s first floating wind farms: three turbines mounted on platforms moored to the seabed, in service since 2020. Floating turbines make it possible to generate power where the sea is too deep for fixed foundations. For the fleet it only matters for boats heading south close to the Iberian coast.
