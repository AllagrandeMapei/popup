Madeira watch zone

Around Madeira, the seabed drops quickly to great depths close to the coast. These waters are rich in food and visited all year round by cetaceans, including sperm whales and rorquals. The zone is not off limits, but it warns skippers that cetacean density is higher. At high speed, hitting a cetacean can seriously injure the animal and damage the boat, sometimes to the point of retiring from the race.

Category: Biodiversity protectionAreas where navigation is regulated to protect fragile ecosystems, cetaceans and sea turtles. Some are closed to the fleet, others flag zones to know and respect.
