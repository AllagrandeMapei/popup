Azores cetacean protection zone

The Azores are a key place for cetaceans: they breed and feed here, and pass through on their migration between Arctic waters, where they feed in summer, and tropical waters, where they breed in winter. The zone was defined by the Share the Ocean scientific consortium using scientific data, statistical models and collision reports.

Category: Biodiversity protectionAreas where navigation is regulated to protect fragile ecosystems, cetaceans and sea turtles. Some are closed to the fleet, others flag zones to know and respect.
