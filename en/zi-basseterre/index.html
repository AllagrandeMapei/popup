Race safety

Basse-Terre buoy exclusion zone

Before the finish, boats must sail around Guadeloupe, rounding a buoy off Basse-Terre. Navigation is prohibited within a 0.25 mile circle (about 460 metres) around this buoy, so competitors can round it safely. Basse-Terre is one of the best spots to follow the final stage of the race.

Category: Race safetyZones set up at the start and finish to protect skippers and the public.
