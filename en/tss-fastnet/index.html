Traffic separation scheme

Fastnet TSS

Off the south western tip of Ireland, the Fastnet TSS organises traffic around the rock of the same name and its lighthouse, famous among sailors for the race named after it. Ships pass here between the Atlantic, the Irish Sea and Irish ports. For the Route du Rhum fleet it lies far north of the route: it only matters in case of extreme weather options to the north west.

Category: Maritime trafficCorridors where merchant ships travel in lanes separated by direction, like on a motorway. During the race they are closed to the boats.
