Agoa cetacean protection zone

Inside the Agoa sanctuary, this zone protects an area where cetaceans are especially numerous. Off Guadeloupe and neighbouring Dominica, groups of sperm whales live all year round, and in winter humpback whales arrive to breed.

Category: Biodiversity protectionAreas where navigation is regulated to protect fragile ecosystems, cetaceans and sea turtles. Some are closed to the fleet, others flag zones to know and respect.
