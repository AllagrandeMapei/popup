Race safety

Arrival assistance zone

Right after the finish, each boat enters a zone where support teams take over: they come aboard, help the skipper and escort the boat to the harbour. After the line the solo sailor can finally stop, but manoeuvring boats up to 32 metres long in a tight space remains tricky. This is why the zone is closed to spectator boats.
