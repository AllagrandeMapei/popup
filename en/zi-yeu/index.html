Yeu-Noirmoutier wind farm construction site

Between the islands of Yeu and Noirmoutier, in Vendée, an offshore wind farm of around sixty turbines is under construction. During the works, the maritime prefecture prohibits navigation and all activity in the construction zone, where large installation vessels operate. Like the Saint-Nazaire wind farm, it only concerns boats heading down the French coast to shelter or make a technical stop.

Category: Energy infrastructureOffshore wind farms and test sites. Turbines, cables and safety zones around the structures make them off limits to racing boats.
