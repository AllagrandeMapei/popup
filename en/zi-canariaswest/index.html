Traffic separation scheme

Canary Islands West TSS

In the channel between Gran Canaria and Tenerife, the archipelago’s second TSS channels traffic between the two most populated islands, with their ports and inter-island ferries. It also protects the waters between the islands, frequented all year round by pilot whales and dolphins. For the fleet there is another difficulty: the high islands create wind shadows to leeward and strong acceleration zones in the channels.
